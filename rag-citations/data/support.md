# NovaDesk Support

## Channels

- In-app chat: available on Pro and Business (business hours, UTC)
- Email: support@novadesk.example for all plans (48-hour SLA on Starter)
- Priority phone: Business only, 24/5 coverage

## Status and incidents

Status updates are posted at status.novadesk.example. Planned maintenance
windows are announced 72 hours ahead for Business customers.

## Knowledge base

Self-serve articles cover Docs permissions, Board automation recipes, and
CRM pipeline templates. Search within the app surfaces the same articles that
power this help corpus.
