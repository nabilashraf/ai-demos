# NovaDesk Pricing

NovaDesk offers three plans. All prices are USD per seat per month, billed annually.

| Plan | Price | Seats | Storage |
|------|-------|-------|---------|
| Starter | $12 | up to 10 | 50 GB |
| Pro | $28 | up to 50 | 500 GB |
| Business | $45 | unlimited* | 2 TB |

\*Business plan seats are billed in packs of 10 after the first 50.

## Refunds

- Starter and Pro: full refund within **14 days** of first charge if usage is under 10% of seat quota.
- Business: refunds require a written request within **7 days**; proration applies after day 7.
- Annual plans may be converted to monthly mid-term with a 10% conversion fee.

## Trials

Every plan includes a 21-day trial. No credit card is required for Starter trials.
